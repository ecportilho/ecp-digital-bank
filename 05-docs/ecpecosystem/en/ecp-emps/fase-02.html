P-01 — Primary Persona (MVP / Beachhead)
Lucas Ferreira, 28 years old — Designer/Dev Freelancer (MEI)

Segment: Digital Freelancer MEI | Income: R$ 5,000–8,000/month (variable) | CNPJ: MEI for 2 years

Context: Works 100% on computer, serves 3–5 simultaneous clients. Uses PF account (Nubank) for everything — personal and professional mixed. Issues invoices via the MEI portal but does not track receivables.

Frustrations: Mixes PF and PJ expenses in the same account. Doesn’t know how much is left from the business. Needs to grab the phone for every Pix. Tried to open a PJ account but gave up due to bureaucracy. Uses spreadsheet for financial control (updates 1x per month).

“I know I should separate the accounts, but opening a PJ account is insane bureaucracy. If I could do it in 3 minutes, without leaving the computer, I would have done it yesterday.”

Key Insights

6 insights extracted from competitive analysis, SEBRAE data and digital freelancer behavior.

INS-01 — High confidence
No PJ bank offers a native web-first experience — the gap is total
All PJ competitors (Inter PJ, Cora, Conta Azul, C6 Business) are mobile-first or have limited web. MEIs who work on computer are forced to use their phone for banking operations. First-mover window of 12–18 months.
INS-02 — High confidence
PF/PJ mixing is the biggest silent pain — 72% of MEIs don’t separate
SEBRAE data shows 72% of MEIs use personal accounts to receive from clients. Native PF-PJ integration with instant toggle solves a problem most don’t even perceive as a problem until they see the solution.
INS-03 — High confidence
PJ onboarding is the #1 barrier — bureaucracy makes them give up
Opening a PJ account at competitors takes 3–7 days with extensive documentation. By leveraging the KYC already validated in the ECP Bank PF account, we can reduce to < 3 minutes with zero additional documents.
INS-04 — Medium confidence
Freelancers give up on formal billing because invoices are expensive
Traditional PJ banks charge R$ 3–8 per issued invoice. Freelancers prefer to send Pix via WhatsApp and lose control. Free invoice with embedded Pix QR is a direct activation differentiator.
INS-05 — Medium confidence
Multi-user control is a barrier for micro-businesses with employees
MEs with 1–5 employees need to give financial access with clear limits. Most digital PJ banks do not offer granular RBAC. For the MVP, 3 profiles (Admin, Financial, Viewer) cover 90% of scenarios.
INS-06 — Medium confidence
Real-time cash flow view is what retains
MEIs who see where the company’s money goes have 2x more retention (competitor benchmarks). PJ dashboard with inflows vs outflows, pending charges and projected balance is what transforms a tool into a primary bank.

User Stories (BDD/Gherkin) — Top 3 Features

US-001 M
As a MEI, I want to enter my CNPJ and see the company data filled in automatically, so I don’t need to type everything manually
Feature: Automatic CNPJ lookup Scenario: Valid and active CNPJ Given I am on the PJ account opening screen When I enter CNPJ "12.345.678/0001-95" And I click "Look up" Then the system validates the CNPJ (module 11) And automatically fills in company name, legal type and address And displays the data for confirmation Scenario: Invalid CNPJ Given I am on the PJ account opening screen When I enter a CNPJ with incorrect check digits Then the system displays error "Invalid CNPJ" And does not advance to the next step
Rules: BR-12 (active CNPJ + CPF validated in PF), BR-14 (CNPJ module 11 validation)
US-004 M
As a MEI, I want to issue a billing invoice by filling in only the client data and amount, so I can charge professionally without bureaucracy
Feature: 2-step invoice issuance Scenario: Issuance with intelligent defaults Given I am on the "New Charge" screen When I fill in name "Carlos Mendes", CPF, amount R$ 2,500.00 And I select due date for 30 days Then the system pre-configures 1% monthly interest and 2% late fee And generates preview with barcode + Pix QR Code And displays buttons "Send by email" and "Copy link" Scenario: Insufficient balance does not block issuance Given my PJ balance is R$ 0.00 When I issue an invoice for R$ 5,000.00 Then the invoice is issued normally Because invoice issuance does not depend on balance
Rules: BR-08 (UUID idempotency), BR-09 (values in cents), BR-14 (30d validity, 1% interest, 2% late fee), BR-15 (receipt with CNPJ)
US-007 M
As a MEI, I want to transfer money between my personal and PJ accounts in 2 clicks, so I can separate finances without bureaucracy
Feature: PF↔PJ Transfer Scenario: PJ → PF transfer with sufficient balance Given my PJ balance is R$ 8,750.00 And my PF balance is R$ 3,200.00 When I select direction "PJ → PF" And enter amount R$ 1,000.00 And confirm the transfer Then PJ balance updates to R$ 7,750.00 And PF balance updates to R$ 4,200.00 And transaction is recorded with idempotency_key Scenario: Insufficient balance Given my PJ balance is R$ 500.00 When I try to transfer R$ 1,000.00 to PF Then the system blocks with message "Insufficient balance"
Rules: BR-04 (insufficient balance blocks), BR-08 (idempotency), BR-09 (cents), BR-13 (instant, same ownership)

Assessment of Cagan’s 4 Risks

Customer Value Approved w/ reservations
Confidence: Medium
Supporting evidence: 23.5M MEIs+MEs, 48% dissatisfied, no web-first + PF-PJ + AI competitor. Personas validate real pains.

Reservation: 4 HIGH assumptions (A-01, A-03, A-05, A-08) not yet tested with real users. Validate in first 30 days post-launch.
Business Value Approved w/ reservations
Confidence: Medium
Impacted KRs: Pix usage (low risk), activation (medium), invoices (medium), D30 retention (HIGHEST RISK).

Reservation: Revenue model (float + future credit) viable but thin in Year 1. D30 retention is the critical KR — needs retention playbook in MVP.
Usability Approved
Confidence: High
6 hypotheses validated via low-fi and high-fi prototypes. Proven patterns (Nubank PJ toggle, desktop banking). Component library reused from PF.

4 friction points: ProfileSwitcher context, invoice fields, RBAC comprehension, empty chart — all with clear mitigations.
Technical Feasibility Approved
Confidence: High
Identical stack to ecp-digital-bank PF (Fastify 5 + SQLite3 + React 18.3). High reuse of components, utils and auth infra. Tech spec details 12 tables, 35 endpoints, seed data.

5 challenges: shared JWT, SQLite concurrency, FEBRABAN barcode, CNPJ lookup, 35 endpoints — all manageable. Estimate: 3–4 weeks.

Conditions to Proceed

  • C-01: Instrument assumptions A-01 and A-08 for measurement from day 1 of launch
  • C-02: Include retention playbook (weekly digest, alerts, invoice reminders) in MVP scope
  • C-03: Implement empty-states for Dashboard, Cash Flow and Charges List
  • C-04: Pre-fill invoice defaults (interest/late fee) and profile descriptions in InviteModal
  • C-05: Plan 30-day post-launch validation cycle with go/no-go criteria
