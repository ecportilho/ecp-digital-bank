Product Vision
The web-first digital bank that automates business financial management with intelligence

For Brazilian micro-entrepreneurs who lose time and money with banks that ignore their business needs, ECP Emps is the only one that integrates personal (PF) and business (PJ) accounts in one place with AI-powered automatic categorization and a native desktop experience.

23.5 million companies in Brazil — and most MEIs and micro-businesses still manage finances with spreadsheets, bad banks, and confusion between personal and business accounts. ECP Emps exists to eliminate that mess.

North Star Metric
EFAS
Financially Active Companies per Week
Number of companies with 3+ financial operations in the last 7 days. Captures the moment when the company uses ECP Emps as its primary bank.

Synthetic Personas

Primary Persona
Lucas — Digital Freelancer MEI

28 years old, designer/dev freelancer, earns R$5,000–8,000/month. Works 100% on computer, has been a sole proprietor (MEI) for 2 years. Uses personal account for everything and gets confused between personal and business expenses. Wants to separate personal/business without bureaucracy.

Frustrations: Mixes personal and business expenses, issues invoices but doesn't track receivables, doesn't know how much is left from the business.

Behavior: Heavy Pix user, does everything on desktop, uses spreadsheets for financial control.

Secondary Persona
Camila — Micro-business Owner ME

34 years old, owns a marketing agency with 3 employees, earns R$25,000/month. Needs to issue billing slips to clients, control team expenses, and separate owner's draw from company cash flow.

Frustrations: Business bank charges abusive fees, no consolidated view, wastes time on manual reconciliation.

Behavior: Uses desktop all day, needs fast financial reports, wants team controls.

Phase Summaries

Phase 01 — Strategic Context
OKRs, North Star and Positioning

Strategic objective: "Make ECP Emps the digital bank that freelancer MEIs trust to separate, organize, and bill", with 4 measurable Key Results (Activation Rate 35%, Billing slips 2+/month, PF↔PJ Toggle 2+/session, D30 Retention 45%). First-mover web-first PJ opportunity window of 12–18 months.
