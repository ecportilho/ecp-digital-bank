Phase 01 — Strategic Context

OKRs, OST (Opportunity Solution Tree), North Star Metric and product principles defined by the Product Manager Agent.

Product Vision

3–5 Year Vision
For Brazilian micro-entrepreneurs who lose time and money with banks that ignore their business needs, ECP Emps is the web-first digital bank that automates business financial management with intelligence, unlike existing PJ digital banks because it is the only one that integrates personal (PF) and business (PJ) accounts in one place with AI-powered automatic categorization and a native desktop experience.

Opportunity window: 12–18 months of first-mover advantage in the web-first PJ segment before competitors react.

Beachhead segment: Digital Freelancer MEI (ages 22–35, digital services, natural desktop affinity, lowest CAC, highest product-market fit for MVP).

Product Principles

01
Desktop first, mobile always
The primary experience is designed for desktop (1280px+), with mobile as a companion.
In practice: Every feature is designed first for large screen; mobile receives a simplified version.
Trade-off: We give up mobile-only market share to gain productivity with the audience that works on computer.
02
Automation beats configuration
The system makes the right choice automatically instead of asking the user to configure.
In practice: Automatic categorization; CNPJ Pix key registered at opening; invoice with intelligent defaults.
Trade-off: We give up flexibility for power users in favor of simplicity for the majority.
03
Financial clarity above all
The entrepreneur must know exactly how much they have, how much comes in and how much goes out — without mental calculation.
In practice: Dashboard shows real profit; clear business categories; cash flow charts always visible.
Trade-off: We prioritize transparency even if it reveals the business is doing poorly.
04
PF and PJ are one entrepreneur
The person and the business coexist; the bank must reflect this reality.
In practice: PF/PJ toggle in the same app; instant PF-PJ transfer; consolidated view.
Trade-off: We give up total independence between PF and PJ in favor of an integrated experience.
05
Free at core, premium for extras
Essential operations (Pix, invoices, account) are free; advanced features may be premium.
In practice: Unlimited Pix for free, free invoices, no monthly account fee.
Trade-off: We give up immediate per-operation revenue in favor of adoption and volume.
06
Entrepreneur’s data belongs to the entrepreneur
Full access and export of financial data, without lock-in.
In practice: Statement export in CSV/PDF, downloadable reports, open API (future).
Trade-off: We make client departure easy, betting that superior experience ensures retention.
07
Security without unnecessary friction
Protect the entrepreneur without them feeling like they’re fighting against their own bank.
In practice: Additional confirmation only above R$ 5K; automatic nighttime limit; RBAC by profile.
Trade-off: We accept that smaller limits pass without extra confirmation in favor of fluidity.

Issues & Open Items

BLOCKING — KR-03 (Toggle PF↔PJ) There is no analytics_events table in the tech_spec to track PF/PJ toggles on the frontend. There is also no definition of “session” in the schema. Cross-app tracking (PJ→PF on port 5173) adds complexity.
Remediation: Add analytics_events table to tech_spec or extend pj_audit_logs with action='profile_switch'.
LOW — KR-02 (Invoices) Installment invoices (type='installment') may inflate the count. Formula must filter parent_invoice_id IS NULL.
LOW — KR-04 (D30 Retention) No explicit PJ login tracking. Using pj_audit_logs as proxy requires passive actions (GET /pj/dashboard) to generate log.
