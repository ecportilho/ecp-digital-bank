Insights and Hypotheses

7 Key Insights

INS-01 — High confidence
Universal web experience gap is the largest available competitive differentiator
All 6 competitors (Nubank, Inter, C6, PicPay, Mercado Pago, Neon) are 100% mobile-only or have extremely limited web. The web interface must be natively designed for 1280px+ screens, leveraging information density, keyboard shortcuts, and multitasking.
INS-02 — High confidence
Absence of financial intelligence in statements is an unmet gap
No competitor offers automatic categorization or proactive insights in statements. Users resort to third-party apps (Organizze, Mobills). The statement must be a "financial intelligence panel", not a chronological list.
INS-03 — High confidence
Onboarding is the critical conversion moment
Time-to-first-action <2h generates 3x more activation. Onboarding must be progressive: minimum fields to create account, first guided action in <2 minutes, progressive feature disclosure.
INS-04 — High confidence
Perceptible security is a prerequisite for trust in a new bank
Every financial operation must have explicit visual security feedback: protection seals, graduated confirmations by value, visible access history, granular virtual card controls.
INS-05 — High confidence
Radical transparency is an emotional differentiator against incumbents
Every transactional screen must show a complete cost breakdown before confirmation. Colloquial and direct language, never legal jargon.
INS-06 — High confidence
Pix on desktop is the most demanded transactional feature
No competitor offers a fluid web Pix experience. Desktop Pix must be as fast as on mobile, optimized for keyboard (tab between fields, Enter to confirm).
INS-07 — Medium confidence
Payroll cycle as organizing axis is a differentiator
All competitors organize statements by calendar month, ignoring that the real financial cycle of a salaried worker goes from payday to the next. Dashboard must offer payroll cycle view.

Synthetic Personas

5 personas representing the 4 market segments. Built by triangulation of IBGE, FEBRABAN data, public reviews and behavioral analysis.

P-01 — Primary Persona (MVP)
Marina Oliveira, 28 — Digital Marketing Analyst

Segment: Young Salaried Professionals | Income: R$5,200/month (CLT) | Payday: 5th

Context: Spends 8–9h/day at computer. Uses Nubank + BB (salary account). Makes 8 Pix/week, always interrupting work to pick up her phone.

Frustrations: Must grab phone for every Pix. Doesn't know how much she spent on food that month. Bank summary organized by calendar month, not payroll cycle. Gave up on manual finance tracking apps.

Goals: Operate the bank without leaving the computer. Understand where money goes effortlessly. Know how much she can spend until the next paycheck.

"I know I earn well for my age, but by the 25th I'm already counting down to next payday. If at least I could see my expenses in an organized way..."

Insights: INS-01, INS-02, INS-03, INS-07 | KRs: KR-01, KR-02, KR-05

Cagan's 4 Risks Assessment

Customer Value
Approved
Confidence: High
Universal competitive gap confirmed: all 6 competitors are 100% mobile-only. Latent demand proven by workaround behavior (Organizze, Mobills). 79% of adults 22–35 use digital channels as primary. 4 value assumptions not yet tested with real users.
Business Value
Approved
Confidence: Medium
9 of 10 opportunities with direct hypothesis coverage. All 5 KRs have at least one associated hypothesis. KR-03 (D30 retention 55%) is the highest-risk gap — no direct retention mechanism in MVP. SAM of R$72 billion validates the market.
Usability
Approved
Confidence: High
Two prototyping rounds approved with no change requests: low-fi (17 screens) and high-fi (17 screens in dark theme). Complete primary flow coverage. Native web-first design for 1280px+. 5 friction points identified, all medium or low impact (except FP-05: Web Notifications opt-in).
Technical Feasibility
Approved
Confidence: Medium
Mature and well-defined stack: React 18.3, Fastify 5.0, SQLite3, TypeScript. 2 high-severity constraints: categorization accuracy (target adjusted to >80%) and absence of real SPI/DICT integration (path via BaaS for go-to-market).

Business Rules (BR-01 to BR-10)

BR-01
Daily Pix limit R$5,000 during business hours (6 AM–10 PM)
BR-02
Nighttime Pix limit R$1,000 per transaction (10 PM–6 AM)
BR-03
Enhanced authentication for transactions above R$2,000
BR-04
Insufficient balance blocks Pix and payments
BR-05
Maximum 5 Pix keys per account (1 CPF + 4 others)
BR-06
Virtual card limit independent of balance; statement closes on the 25th
BR-07
Soft delete — no record is physically deleted
BR-08
Idempotency via idempotency_key in POST requests
BR-09
Values stored in cents (integer), displayed in BRL on front-end
BR-10
Rate limit: maximum 10 Pix per hour per user
